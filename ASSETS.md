## Flags

- **Quaternius**: pack pages are CC0 and reachable, but their Drive download links return "Quota exceeded" for curl. Re-try later from a browser.
- **Poly Pizza**: sampled model (/m/06dagPn-QYn) is **CC-BY 3.0**, not CC0. Do not use Poly Pizza models unless each one is checked for CC0.
- **Kenney animal-pack-redux**: download link returned NONE; birds unresolved.
- **Freesound**: all 5 files verified as "Creative Commons 0" on their pages. Previews are MP3 low-quality; re-download originals from the page if you need quality.
- **Kenney nature-kit** is the workhorse pack (329 GLB files, CC0 per License.txt).
